EuroVelo 10 – Baltic Sea Cycle Route (Poland section, Velo Baltica): bikepacking plan & riding days

The Polish leg of EuroVelo 10 follows the Baltic coast from Świnoujście through Kołobrzeg, Ustka, Łeba and Gdańsk to Elbląg and the Kaliningrad border. Mostly flat and seaside, it ranges from new asphalt paths to sandy forest sections.

About the route

  • The Polish section is about 588 km long and crosses the West Pomeranian, Pomeranian and Warmian-Masurian voivodeships (Polish Wikipedia).
  • The shorter variant without the Hel Peninsula is about 533 km, and the route runs from Świnoujście near the German border to Elbląg (Traseo).
  • In the west it is known as Velo Baltica and shares its course with EuroVelo 13, the Iron Curtain Trail (Wikivoyage; VeloPlanner).

Planning notes

  • Most riders go west to east, starting in Świnoujście (VeloPlanner).
  • Expect a mix of surfaces: new asphalt paths and old railway-line cycleways, but also forest tracks and some sandy stretches in Pomorskie. Allow extra time between Łeba and Rowy.

FAQ

How many days does it take to bikepack EV10 Poland Baltic Coast?

With BikepackPilot's default pace of 110 km per day on asphalt, the 588 km route takes about 5.8 riding days in good conditions and about 8.8 days with moderate headwind and light rain, using the surface mix shown on this page. Rest days are not included.

How long is EV10 Poland Baltic Coast?

about 588 km including the Hel Peninsula; roughly 520–533 km for the shorter Świnoujście–Elbląg main line, from Świnoujście (German border), Poland to Gronowo (Kaliningrad border), Poland.

What surface is EV10 Poland Baltic Coast?
