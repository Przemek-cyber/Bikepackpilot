EuroVelo 9 – The Amber Route: bikepacking plan & riding days

A Baltic-to-Adriatic ride through six countries: from Gdańsk along the Vistula, across Poland to Wrocław, through Moravia to Vienna and down through Slovenia to the Istrian coast.

About the route

  • Runs from Gdańsk on the Baltic Sea to Pula on the Adriatic Sea.
  • Crosses Poland, the Czech Republic, Austria, Slovenia, Italy and Croatia.
  • Main cities on the way include Gdańsk, Poznań, Wrocław, Olomouc, Brno, Vienna, Maribor, Ljubljana and Trieste.
  • Terrain changes a lot: flat river valleys in Poland, the hilly Sudetes, the Moravian Karst and the hills of Istria.
  • Some sections, especially in Slovenia, Italy and Croatia, may run on busier roads.

Planning notes

  • Treat it as several different trips in one: plan Poland, Czechia/Austria and Slovenia/Croatia as separate blocks with their own conditions.
  • Expect more climbing from the Sudetes southwards — add elevation per stage instead of using one average.
  • Summer heat in Istria changes both your daily distance and your water needs; plan hot segments separately.

How many days does it take to bikepack EuroVelo 9?

With BikepackPilot's default pace of 110 km per day on asphalt, the 1,930 km route takes about 18.5 riding days in good conditions and about 27.1 days with moderate headwind and light rain, using the surface mix shown on this page. Rest days are not included.

How long is EuroVelo 9?

about 1,900–1,930 km depending on the source and variant, from Gdańsk, Poland to Pula, Croatia.
