About the route

  • Runs about 320–333 km from Passau to Vienna.
  • Mostly downhill with the river: from around 300 m above sea level in Passau to 158 m in Vienna.
  • Classic 7-stage split: Passau–Schlögen–Linz–Grein–Melk–Krems–Tulln–Vienna.
  • The Wachau between Melk and Krems is widely seen as the most beautiful part.

Planning notes

  • A great first multi-day trip: the challenge is not the terrain but pacing and accommodation.
  • Headwind along the river is the main thing that slows you down – compare the two scenarios below.
  • Many people ride it in 6–7 days with sightseeing; at a bikepacking pace it is much shorter.
