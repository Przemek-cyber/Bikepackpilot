Trans-Dolomiti Bikepacking Loop: bikepacking plan & riding days

A hard 262 km mountain-bike loop from Brixen through the Dolomites, linking forest tracks, dirt roads and singletrack, with options to stay in mountain huts. Ride it clockwise.

About the route

  • Bikepacking.com lists the loop at 262 km with 9,529 m of total ascent, and a high point of 2,422 m.
  • The same source gives the surface as 56% unpaved and 9% singletrack, with 97% rideable by time. It rates the difficulty 7 out of 10.
  • The route is based on a semi-official, non-signposted route found online. It was already popular as a hut-to-hut route, but Bikepacking.com says it hadn't been bikepacked before.
  • Bikepacking.com recommends riding clockwise because the climbs are easier that way. Brixen is the suggested start because it has a train station and all services.
  • A typical split is 5–6 days. One Komoot version breaks it into six stages: Brixen, St. Magdalena, St. Kassian, Selva di Cadore, Sottoguda, Kolfuschg and back to Brixen.
  • Bikepacking.com's editors describe the riding as a mix of forest tracks, coarse dirt roads and flowy trails, with chances to overnight in historic mountain refuges.

Planning notes

  • Bring a mountain bike with front suspension or a lightweight full-suspension bike. The long descents are often rocky, and Bikepacking.com says the route isn't suited to gravel bikes.
  • Expect some hike-a-bike. Komoot's stage descriptions warn that you may need to push your bike on some segments, and rate the stages as hard with very good fitness required.
