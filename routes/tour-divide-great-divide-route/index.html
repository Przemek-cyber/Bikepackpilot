About the route

  • The Tour Divide course runs about 2,750 miles from Banff, Alberta to Antelope Wells, New Mexico.
  • About 90% of the Great Divide Mountain Bike Route is on unpaved roads and trails.
  • Total climbing on the Tour Divide course is around 45,600 m (about 150,000 ft).
  • It passes through Alberta, Montana, Wyoming, Colorado and New Mexico.
  • The race record is under 14 days – most touring riders take far longer.

Planning notes

  • Remote sections with long gaps between services make water and food planning per segment essential.
  • Mountain weather changes fast: plan cold, wet segments in the north and hot, dry segments in New Mexico separately.
  • With this much climbing, enter elevation per stage – it changes your riding days more than distance does.
