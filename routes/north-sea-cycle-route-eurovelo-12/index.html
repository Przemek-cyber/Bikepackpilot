North Sea Cycle Route (EuroVelo 12): bikepacking plan & riding days

EuroVelo 12 is a signposted circuit around the North Sea coast, linking Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, France and the UK. It is billed as the longest signposted cycle route in the world.

About the route

  • The official EuroVelo listing gives the route as 7,250 km; older sources quote about 6,000 km or 5,942 km, so published lengths differ.
  • The route forms a circuit around the North Sea and passes through eight countries, including both England and Scotland in the UK.
  • The German section is roughly 920 km, running from Bad Nieuweschans via Emden, Bremerhaven, Cuxhaven and Hamburg to Ruttebüll at the Danish border, mostly on tarmac.
  • Landscapes range from below-sea-level Netherlands to high cliffs and fjords in Norway.

Planning notes

  • The route is built from national routes, so signs and route names change at each border; check the local numbering as you cross.
  • The southern coast (Netherlands, Belgium, northern France) and the Schleswig-Holstein coast are flat, while Norway and Scotland are much hillier.
  • Expect open, exposed coastline in many places, so wind direction can matter a lot on the dykes and beaches.
