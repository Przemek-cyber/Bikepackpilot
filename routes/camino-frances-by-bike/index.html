About the route

  • About 769 km from Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port to Santiago de Compostela.
  • Main cities on the way include Pamplona, Logroño, Burgos and León.
  • The highest point of the route is about 1,515 m, near Manjarín.
  • Starts with a Pyrenees crossing into Spain at Roncesvalles.

Planning notes

  • Cyclists can follow the walking path or parallel roads – the surface mix changes your daily distance a lot.
  • The Meseta between Burgos and León is exposed: heat and wind matter more than hills there.
  • Plan the Pyrenees and the Galician hills as separate, shorter stages.

FAQ

How many days does it take to bikepack Camino Francés?

With BikepackPilot's default pace of 110 km per day on asphalt, the 769 km route takes about 7.7 riding days in good conditions and about 11.6 days with moderate headwind and light rain, using the surface mix shown on this page. Rest days are not included.

How long is Camino Francés?

about 770–780 km, from Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port, France to Santiago de Compostela, Spain.

What surface is Camino Francés?
