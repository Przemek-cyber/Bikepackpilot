About the route

  • 185 miles (297 km) from Vatersay to the Butt of Lewis.
  • Crosses 10 islands and six causeways and includes two ferries.
  • A waymarked on-road route on mostly quiet roads.
  • Most riders go south to north to use the generally prevailing south-westerly winds.
  • Usually ridden in two to six days.

Planning notes

  • Wind decides your day here more than distance – the plan below shows a calm and a windy scenario.
  • Ferry times fix parts of your schedule: plan the ferry days first, then fill the rest.
  • Shops and water points are sparse on some islands, so mark them per segment.

FAQ

How many days does it take to bikepack Hebridean Way?

With BikepackPilot's default pace of 110 km per day on asphalt, the 297 km route takes about 2.7 riding days in good conditions and about 3.9 days with moderate headwind and light rain, using the surface mix shown on this page. Rest days are not included.

How long is Hebridean Way?

185 miles (297 km), from Vatersay, Outer Hebrides to Butt of Lewis, Outer Hebrides.

What surface is Hebridean Way?

For this plan we assume 100% asphalt. In BikepackPilot you set the surface for each 50 km segment yourself.
