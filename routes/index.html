Bikepacking route plans

How many days does a famous route really take? Each plan splits the route into 50 km segments and estimates realistic riding days for good and tough conditions – the same way BikepackPilot plans your own trip.

Camino Francés by Bike

The most famous Camino de Santiago route, from the French Pyrenees across northern Spain to Santiago de Compostela.

769 km · ≈8+ riding days · France, Spain

Danube Cycle Path – Passau to Vienna

One of Europe's most popular cycle routes: downstream along the Danube through the Upper Danube valley, Linz and the Wachau to Vienna.

333 km · ≈3+ riding days · Germany, Austria

EuroVelo 6 – Atlantic to Black Sea (The Rivers Route)

EuroVelo 6 follows the Loire, a short stretch of the Rhine and nearly the whole Danube from France's Atlantic coast to Romania's Black Sea coast. It is mostly flat, popular, and crosses ten countries.

4,700 km · ≈46+ riding days · France, Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Slovakia, Hungary, Croatia, Serbia, Bulgaria, Romania

EuroVelo 9 – The Amber Route

A Baltic-to-Adriatic ride through six countries: from Gdańsk along the Vistula, across Poland to Wrocław, through Moravia to Vienna and down through Slovenia to the Istrian coast.

1,930 km · ≈18+ riding days · Poland, Czech Republic, Austria, Slovenia, Italy, Croatia

Green Velo – Eastern Cycling Trail

Poland's longest cycling route, running through five eastern voivodeships, three national parks in Podlasie and long stretches of forest and river valleys.

1,981 km · ≈19+ riding days · Poland

Hebridean Way Cycling Route

An island-hopping road route through the Outer Hebrides – 10 islands, six causeways and two ferries, from Vatersay to the Butt of Lewis.

297 km · ≈3+ riding days · United Kingdom (Scotland)

Tour Divide – Great Divide Mountain Bike Route

The classic North American off-road bikepacking route along the Continental Divide, from the Canadian Rockies to the Mexican border.

4,425 km · ≈56+ riding days · Canada, USA
